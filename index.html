Drawing in. DXF out.

A focused tool for turning dimensioned PDFs, PNGs, JPGs and workshop sketches into verified DXF geometry. The production workflow will keep human confirmation between interpretation and export.

1. Upload PDF, image or phone photo of a dimensioned drawing.
2. Confirm Click through detected dimensions, edit mistakes and resolve ambiguity.
3. Export Generate clean DXF geometry for the required cut or fabrication workflow.
